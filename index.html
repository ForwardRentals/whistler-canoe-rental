Why a canoe

The easiest way onto Whistler's water.

Whistler has five swimmable lakes within a few minutes of the Village, and the hardest part of paddling them is getting a boat there. So we bring it.

A canoe takes up to three people, nobody needs to stand, and there is room for a cooler, a dog or a small kid in the middle. It is the relaxed way to spend an afternoon on Alta or Lost Lake.

Whistler Canoe Rental is run by Squamish Canoe Rental, a locally owned paddling outfit that covers the whole Sea to Sky from Squamish to Pemberton.

Launch spots

Pick your lake.

We deliver to every main Whistler lake, plus Pemberton. Not sure which one? Ask when you book and we'll point you at the calmest water for the day.

Alta Lake

The central Whistler lake, with put-ins at Rainbow, Lakeside and Wayside parks. Calmest in the morning before the afternoon breeze picks up.

Lost Lake

Walking distance from the Village. No motorboats and lots of swim breaks: the family favourite.

Green Lake

Glacial turquoise water under Wedge Mountain. Bigger, colder and windier; a canoe handles it better than a paddleboard.

Nita Lake

Small, quiet and usually glassy. A sunrise or sunset paddle.

Alpha Lake

Shallow and calm in Creekside, with a beach park. A forgiving first canoe outing.

One Mile Lake, Pemberton

Warmer water 25 minutes north of Whistler, away from the summer crowds.

River of Golden Dreams

Float from Alta Lake down to Green Lake. We drop the canoe at Rainbow Park and pick it up at Meadow Park.

FAQ

Good to know.

Which Whistler lakes can I paddle?

Alta, Lost, Green, Nita and Alpha lakes, the Whistler RV Park, and One Mile Lake in Pemberton. You can also paddle the River of Golden Dreams from Alta Lake down to Green Lake with a shuttle.

Is the canoe delivered?

Yes. The canoe is dropped at the launch you choose and picked up afterwards, so you don't need a roof rack or a truck.

How many people fit in a canoe?

Up to three paddlers per canoe, which makes it the easy choice for families with young kids or anyone who would rather sit than stand on a paddleboard.

What's included?

Canoe, two or three paddles, PFDs (life jackets), a bailer and safety whistle, plus local advice on where and when to paddle.

How much does it cost?

Whistler delivery is priced by launch spot. Current prices and open dates are shown live on Rented Local when you pick your day.

When is canoe season in Whistler?

Late June through early September is prime. The lakes warm up by July, and the River of Golden Dreams usually runs best from late June through July while the water is high.

Do I need experience?

Not for Alta, Lost, Nita or Alpha. Green Lake is bigger and catches more wind, so some canoe experience is recommended there.
